MODEL

How demand becomes power

The real hourly activity pattern is normalized between 0 and 1. Equipment power is interpolated between the assumed idle and peak values, then increased by a 20% site-overhead factor.

idle + (peak − idle) × utilization

RESULT

Traffic increases the resilience risk

The modeled battery meets the target overnight and during lower-load periods, but falls below four hours from 9:00 AM through 8:00 PM. At the 1:00 PM traffic peak, site power reaches 3.0 kW and estimated runtime drops to 2.4 hours.
