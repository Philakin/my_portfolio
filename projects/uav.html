Overview

A senior capstone with Raytheon Technologies focused on the communication and telemetry subsystems of an unmanned aerial vehicle. The team's job was to make the link measurably better while meeting aerospace standards.

What I did

I improved UAV communication performance by 20% by optimizing the telemetry pipeline and the ROS system driving it. Working biweekly with a group of Raytheon engineers, I helped integrate the telemetry systems so they met the program's aerospace requirements.

We validated the communication and telemetry subsystems across more than ten autonomous flight tests, analyzing real-time performance, sensor accuracy, and overall reliability under actual flight conditions.
